## 2026-10-25: Contractors and mutual aid are about half of hurricane restoration cost
- Status: checking
- Evidence: FPL 2024 final approved costs (S001). Contractors 48% (Debby), 53% (Helene), 54% (Milton). Utility payroll 4% to 11%.
- Tags: contractors, cost_mix, FL
- Post-worthy: yes, once Duke Energy Florida and TECO categories are added for comparison
- Open question: how much of "contractors" is mutual aid versus contract crews? Florida orders don't split it.

## 2026-10-25: Logistics share rises with storm size
- Status: checking
- Evidence: FPL logistics 11.1% (Debby, $107M), 13.4% (Helene, $193M), 16.5% (Milton, $876M) (S001).
- Tags: logistics, cost_mix, scale
- Post-worthy: yes, but three storms from one utility is not a pattern. Needs at least two more utilities.

## 2026-10-25: Early storm cost estimates miss by up to 20%, and in both directions
- Status: checking
- Evidence: FPL petition vs final: Debby -20.5%, Helene +9.0%, Milton -5.2% (S001, S003). Debby logistics +37.5% while contractors -25.9%.
- Tags: estimates, ETR, regulatory
- Post-worthy: yes. Duke Energy Florida also revised Helene capital from $77.1M to $27.3M (S004).
